FeatLens connects functional requirements with dependencies dispersed across a repository, reducing the need for multi-step online LLM exploration.

- Builds an offline feature–function index and expands entities along call, use, and ownership relations.
- Combines semantic relevance and structural heuristics to construct a personalized PageRank restart distribution and select context under a fixed budget.
- Makes **zero LLM calls during online dependency retrieval**. Offline indexing and downstream code generation are separate stages; this is not a claim that the entire pipeline is LLM-free.
- At the same **Top-15 budget**, improved dependency recall over UniXcoder RAG by **11.2 percentage points on DevEval** and **17.4 percentage points on EvoCodeBench**.
- On DevEval, achieved **55.03% generation Pass@1 with GPT-5-mini**.

**Status:** Under review at FSE 2027; not yet accepted. The results above follow the submission experiments reported in my CV.
