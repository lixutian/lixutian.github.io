## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at the School of Computer Science, Peking University, with a focus on software engineering. I joined the direct-entry Ph.D. program in September 2025 and expect to graduate in June 2030.

I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology in June 2025. <!-- Over my first three undergraduate years, my average credit-weighted score was **92.7/100**, and I ranked **1st out of 162** students in my major. --> I received the **National Scholarship twice**, as well as the Beijing Outstanding Student and Beijing Outstanding Graduate honors.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on **code intelligence and LLM agents**, especially connecting natural-language feature requirements with repository-level code. My work spans:

- Feature-driven code evolution and software maintenance.
- Code dependency retrieval, static program analysis, and graph-based context selection.
- Feature-oriented repository summarization and code comprehension.
